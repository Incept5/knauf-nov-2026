SITE INCIDENT REPORT  IR-0912       Date: 2 November 2026
Site: Wohnanlage Lindenhof, building B, level 3
What happened: a stack of jointing compound buckets stored by the lift shaft tipped over when the lift was
called; no injuries. Two buckets split and compound reached the stairwell, creating a slip risk until cleaned at 11:20.
Contributing factors: pallet stored on uneven ground, no barrier, storage area not agreed at the morning briefing.
Immediate actions: area cleaned, pallet moved to ground floor store. Open: storage plan for all levels (owner: site manager),
toolbox talk on stacking limits (owner: H&S officer, due 9 November).
