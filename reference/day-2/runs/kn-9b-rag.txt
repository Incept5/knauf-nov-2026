The maximum stud spacing for a single-layer partition with standard board is 600 mm between stud centres.
Double-layer partitions also use 600 mm centres.

For fire-rated systems the spacing is tighter. System FR-90 (75 mm studs, double layer on both sides, type F board)
must be built with studs at 400 mm centres. Never widen the spacing of a fire-rated system on site, because the fire
test result is only valid for the tested layout.

## What stud spacing should I use?

For all partitions, including fire-rated ones, the maximum stud spacing is 625 mm between centres. This allows a
standard 1250 mm wide board to be fixed on a stud at its edges and in the middle.

## Can I use standard board instead of type F?

No. Replacing even one layer of type F board with standard board voids the system rating. The same applies to using
thinner board or leaving out the insulation that the system certificate requires.

## 3. Maximum partition height

With 50 mm studs at 600 mm centres the partition may be up to 3.0 m high. With 75 mm studs at 600 mm centres it may be
up to 4.5 m high. For fire-rated systems, take the height limit from the system certificate, not from this table.

## 4. Fixing the boards

Screw spacing is 250 mm along board edges and 300 mm in the field of the board. Use 25 mm screws for the first layer and
35 mm screws for the second layer. Drive screws so that the head sits just below the paper surface without breaking it.

# Fire Protection Systems: Frequently Asked Questions

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, technical service. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
FAQ no. 12/2023. Last reviewed: May 2023.

## What fire resistance can I get with plasterboard?

Type F fire-rated board (15 mm) is used in tested systems. System FR-60 gives 60 minutes with a single layer of type F
board on each side of a steel stud frame. System FR-90 gives up to 90 minutes and needs a double layer of type F board
on both sides of a 75 mm steel stud frame. Only the complete system is tested; a board alone has no fire rating.
