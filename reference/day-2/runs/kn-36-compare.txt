Q1 RAG: What is the maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition?
The sources provide conflicting information regarding the maximum stud spacing. The installation guide states that the maximum spacing for a single-layer partition with standard board is 600 mm [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2], while the fire protection FAQ claims the maximum spacing for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, is 625 mm [fire_protection_faq.md#2].

Q1 FULL: What is the maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition?
The maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition is 600 mm between stud centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. Additionally, the fire protection FAQ notes that the maximum stud spacing for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, is 625 mm between centres to allow for standard board fixing [fire_protection_faq.md].

Q2 RAG: Within how many hours must damaged goods be reported to Customer Service?
Damaged goods must be reported within 48 hours [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md#1]. This is a change from the previous version 2.x policy, which allowed 5 working days [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md#1].

Q2 FULL: Within how many hours must damaged goods be reported to Customer Service?
Damaged goods must be reported within 48 hours [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md]. This is a change from the previous version, which allowed 5 working days [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md]. Note that the delivery SLA documents mention a 5 working day window for reporting damage on the delivery note, but the specific returns policy overrides this with a 48-hour limit for written reports to Customer Service [delivery_sla_2025.md], [delivery_sla_2026.md], [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md].

Q3 RAG: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?

Q3 FULL: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?

Under **delivery_sla_2025.md** (Version 2.1, valid from 1 January 2025), delivery takes within **5 working days** [delivery_sla_2025.md].

However, **delivery_sla_2026.md** (Version 3.0, effective 1 September 2026) supersedes the earlier version and states that delivery takes within **3 working days** for items in stock at the regional depot [delivery_sla_2026.md].

Q4 RAG: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To achieve 90 minutes of fire resistance using System FR-90, you must use Type F fire-rated board (15 mm) in double layers on both sides of a 75 mm steel stud frame [fire_protection_faq.md#1]. The studs must be spaced at 400 mm centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2]. For fixing the boards, use 35 mm screws for the second layer with spacing of 250 mm along edges and 300 mm in the field; however, note that one source states the maximum stud spacing for all partitions is 625 mm, but specifically mandates 400 mm for this fire-rated system [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2][fire_protection_faq.md#2].

Q4 FULL: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance (System FR-90), you need the following components and specifications:

*   **Boards:** Type F fire-rated plasterboard (15 mm thick) [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md], [fire_protection_faq.md].
*   **Studs:** 75 mm steel studs [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md], [plasterboard_installation_guide.md].
*   **Stud Spacing:** 400 mm centers [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md], [plasterboard_installation_guide.md].
*   **Configuration:** Double layer of Type F board on both sides of the stud frame [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md], [fire_protection_faq.md].
*   **Screw Spacing:** 250 mm along board edges and 300 mm in the field for each layer [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md], [plasterboard_installation_guide.md].

Note that only the complete tested system provides the fire rating; using standard board or altering these specifications voids the rating [fire_protection_faq.md].
