Q1 RAG: What is the maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition?
For a standard single-layer partition, the maximum stud spacing is 600 mm between centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2]. However, another source states that for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, the maximum stud spacing is 625 mm between centres to allow a standard 1250 mm wide board to be fixed on a stud at its edges and in the middle [fire_protection_faq.md#2]. These sources disagree on the specific maximum spacing (600 mm vs. 625 mm).

Q1 FULL: What is the maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition?
For a standard single-layer partition with standard board, the maximum stud spacing is 600 mm between centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. This aligns with the general rule that the maximum stud spacing for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, is 625 mm, though fire-rated systems require tighter spacing of 400 mm or 600 mm depending on the specific system [fire_protection_faq.md] [plasterboard_installation_guide.md].

Q2 RAG: Within how many hours must damaged goods be reported to Customer Service?
Damaged goods must be reported within **48 hours** according to the policy effective from July 1, 2026 [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md#1]. Claims reported after this window may be refused [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md#3]. This is a change from previous versions (version 2.x and the 2025 SLA), which allowed a reporting window of 5 working days [delivery_sla_2025.md#5] or 48 hours depending on the specific document version cited, but the current active policy specifies 48 hours [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md#1].

Q2 FULL: Within how many hours must damaged goods be reported to Customer Service?
Damaged goods must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery, according to the *Returns and Damaged Goods Policy* version 3.0 effective 1 July 2026 [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md]. This policy replaced the previous window of 5 working days found in earlier versions [returns_and_damaged_goods_policy.md].

Q3 RAG: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?
Under the current valid agreement (Version 3.0, effective 1 September 2026), items in stock at the regional depot are delivered within **3 working days** after the order is confirmed [delivery_sla_2026.md#1]. Previously, under Version 2.1 (valid from 1 January 2025), this timeframe was **5 working days** [delivery_sla_2025.md#1].

Q3 FULL: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?
The number of working days for in-stock deliveries depends on which Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA) applies:

*   **Version 2.1** (valid from 1 January 2025) states delivery takes **5 working days** [delivery_sla_2025.md].
*   **Version 3.0** (effective 1 September 2026, superseding earlier versions) states delivery takes **3 working days** [delivery_sla_2026.md].

Q4 RAG: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To build a wall with 90 minutes of fire resistance (System FR-90), you must use type F board in double layers on both sides of a 75 mm steel stud frame [fire_protection_faq.md#1]. The studs must be spaced at 400 mm centres, and the screw spacing should be 250 mm along edges and 300 mm in the field [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2]. Note that standard board cannot replace type F board, as doing so voids the system rating [fire_protection_faq.md#2].

Q4 FULL: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance (System FR-90), you must use **Type F boards** (15 mm thick) in a **double-layer** configuration on both sides of **75 mm steel studs**. The studs must be spaced at **400 mm centres**, and screws should be placed at **250 mm along edges** and **300 mm in the field** [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md][fire_protection_faq.md][plasterboard_installation_guide.md].

Q5 RAG: Wie viele Paletten Brandschutzplatten dürfen im Lager maximal übereinander gestapelt werden?
Im Lager dürfen maximal **8 Paletten** Brandschutzplatten übereinander gestapelt werden [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md#4].

Q5 FULL: Wie viele Paletten Brandschutzplatten dürfen im Lager maximal übereinander gestapelt werden?
Im Lager dürfen maximal 8 Paletten Brandschutzplatten übereinander gestapelt werden [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md]. Auf der Baustelle gelten strengere Regeln, wonach nur maximal 2 Paletten hoch gestapelt werden dürfen [site_safety_procedure.md].

Q6 RAG: How soon after an injury on site must the H&S officer be called?
For any injury or a near miss that could have caused serious injury, the site manager must call the H&S officer within **2 hours** [site_safety_procedure.md#3].

Q6 FULL: How soon after an injury on site must the H&S officer be called?
According to the Site Safety Procedure (Version 4, effective 15 June 2026) [site_safety_procedure.md], if there is any injury or a near miss that could have caused serious injury, the site manager must call the H&S officer within **2 hours**.

Q7 RAG: Who has to approve a production release, and when is the change freeze?
A production release requires two approvals recorded in the release ticket: one from the Release Manager (who verifies tests are green, a rollback plan exists, and notes are written) and one from a Product Owner of an affected area [release_process_note.md#2]. Developers cannot approve their own changes, and if an approver is absent, their deputy may sign instead [release_process_note.md#3]. A change freeze occurs from 15 December 2026 until 6 January 2027, during which only hotfixes are permitted [release_process_note.md#4].

Q7 FULL: Who has to approve a production release, and when is the change freeze?
A production release requires approval from two people: the Release Manager and one Product Owner of an affected area [release_process_note.md]. There is a change freeze in place from 15 December 2026 until 6 January 2027, during which no normal releases go to production [release_process_note.md].
