For a standard single-layer partition with standard board, the maximum stud spacing is 600 mm between centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. This aligns with the general rule that the maximum stud spacing for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, is 625 mm, though fire-rated systems require tighter spacing of 400 mm [fire_protection_faq.md].
