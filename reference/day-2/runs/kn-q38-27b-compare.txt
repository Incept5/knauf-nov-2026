Q1 RAG: What is the maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition?
The maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer partition with standard board is 600 mm between stud centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2]. However, the fire protection FAQ states that for all partitions, including fire-rated ones, the maximum stud spacing is 625 mm [fire_protection_faq.md#2]. These sources disagree on the maximum spacing for standard partitions.

Q3 RAG: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?
The answer depends on which SLA version applies, as the sources disagree:

- Under the 2025 SLA (Version 2.1, valid from 1 January 2025), in-stock items are delivered within **5 working days** [delivery_sla_2025.md#1].
- Under the 2026 SLA (Version 3.0, effective 1 September 2026), in-stock items are delivered within **3 working days** [delivery_sla_2026.md#1].

The 2026 version explicitly supersedes all earlier SLAs, including the 2025 version [delivery_sla_2026.md#1]. Therefore, for orders on or after 1 September 2026, the delivery time is **3 working days**.

Q3 FULL: How many working days does delivery take for items that are in stock at the depot?

Q4 RAG: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance (System FR-90), you must use a 75 mm steel stud frame with a double layer of 15 mm Type F fire-rated board on both sides [fire_protection_faq.md#1]. The studs must be spaced at 400 mm centres, as this specific spacing is required for the FR-90 system and cannot be widened on site [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2]. For fixing the boards, use 25 mm screws for the first layer and 35 mm screws for the second layer, spaced at 250 mm along the edges and 300 mm in the field of the board [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#3]. Note that the sources disagree on maximum stud spacing: the installation guide specifies 400 mm for FR-90 [plasterboard_installation_guide.md#2], while the FAQ states a general maximum of 625 mm for all partitions [fire_protection_faq.md#2]; however, the stricter 400 mm limit applies to this fire-rated system.

Q4 FULL: What do I need to build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance: which boards, studs, stud spacing and screw spacing?
To build a wall with 90 minutes fire resistance (System FR-90), you must use Type F fire-rated boards (15 mm thick) in a double layer on both sides of a 75 mm steel stud frame [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md]. The studs must be spaced at 400 mm centres, and this spacing must not be widened on site [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md] [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. Screws should be placed at 250 mm intervals along the board edges and 300 mm in the field, with the second layer staggered by at least 400 mm [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md] [plasterboard_installation_guide.md].
