The maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition is 600 mm between centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. However, the fire protection FAQ notes that the general maximum stud spacing for all partitions is 625 mm to allow for fixing at edges and middle [fire_protection_faq.md].
