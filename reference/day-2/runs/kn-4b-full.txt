The maximum stud spacing for a standard single-layer plasterboard partition is **600 mm** between centres [plasterboard_installation_guide.md]. This applies to both 50 mm and 75 mm studs when using standard board, whereas fire-rated systems require tighter spacing (400 mm) [plasterboard_installation_guide.md], [datenblatt_brandschutzplatte_typ_f.md].
