# Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, Logistics. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
Version 3.0, effective 1 September 2026. This version supersedes all earlier SLAs, including version 2.1 of 2025.

## 1. Delivery times

- Items in stock at the regional depot: delivery within 3 working days after the order is confirmed.
- Items not in stock: delivery within 10 working days after the order is confirmed. We confirm the date in writing
  within 1 working day.
- Back-ordered items: we notify the customer within 1 working day and give a firm date. The date on the delivery note is binding.

## 2. Delivery windows

Deliveries take place Monday to Friday between 06:30 and 15:00. Crane lorry deliveries are booked in a 2-hour slot and must
be requested at least 48 hours in advance. Standard lorry deliveries are given a morning or afternoon window.

## 3. Unloading and waiting time

The first 60 minutes at the site are free. After that, a waiting charge of 85 euros per 30 minutes is applied. The customer
must make sure that access is clear, the ground is firm and a person is present to sign the delivery note.

## 4. Late delivery

If a delivery arrives later than the confirmed date, a credit of 2% of the order value is given for each working day of delay,
capped at 10%. The customer must claim within 10 working days of the delivery date. A delay caused by closed access or by
the weather is not counted.

## 5. Delivery note and checks

The driver hands over a delivery note listing each item and the quantity. The customer checks quantity and condition at
unloading. Damage or shortage must be written on the delivery note and reported under the returns and damaged goods policy.

## 6. Service level target

We target 95% of deliveries on time, measured monthly. The result is reported to key customers in the first week of the
following month. If the monthly result is below 90%, a review meeting is held within 10 working days.

## 7. Changes and cancellations

An order can be changed free of charge until 24 hours before dispatch. After that, a change fee of 40 euros applies.
Cancelling a crane lorry slot less than 24 hours ahead is charged at 150 euros.
