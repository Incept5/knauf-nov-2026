# Returns and Damaged Goods Policy

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, Customer Service. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
Version 3.0, effective 1 July 2026. This version replaces all version 2.x policies.

## 1. What changed in version 3.0

The reporting window for damaged goods and short deliveries is now 48 hours. In version 2.x it was 5 working days.
Customers who still quote the old window should be told about the change politely, and the new window applies to all
deliveries made on or after 1 July 2026.

## 2. Damaged goods

Check every delivery at the time of unloading. If something is damaged:

- Write "damaged" and the quantity on the delivery note before the driver leaves, and have the site foreman sign it.
- Take photos of the pallet, the label and each damaged item.
- Report the damage in writing to returns@alpenbau.example within 48 hours of delivery, quoting the delivery note number.

Claims reported after 48 hours may be refused. Damage that is not noted on the delivery note is only accepted if the
photos clearly show transport damage.

Water-damaged board cannot be reused, even if it has dried. Do not install it. We arrange collection of the damaged
goods and a credit note or a replacement, as the customer prefers.

## 3. Short deliveries

If fewer items arrive than the delivery note shows, report this in writing within 48 hours. If the shortage is
already noted on the delivery note as a back-order, no claim is needed; the back-order date is binding in the delivery SLA.

## 4. Credit notes

A credit note is issued within 10 working days after we have inspected or collected the goods. A credit never exceeds the
net value of the affected items. Transport costs are credited only if the damage was caused during transport.

## 5. Standard returns of undamaged goods

Undamaged, unopened goods in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15% of
the net value is charged. Goods cut to size, special orders and part pallets that are no longer complete cannot be returned.
Returns of more than 20 boards need a collection appointment, which is charged at 60 euros per pallet.

## 6. Disputes

If a customer disagrees with the decision, a second reviewer in Customer Service looks at the file within 5 working days.
Include all photos, the signed delivery note and the correspondence.

## 7. Contact

Returns desk: returns@alpenbau.example. Telephone line is open Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 16:00.
