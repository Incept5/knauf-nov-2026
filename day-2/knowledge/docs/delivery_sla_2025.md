# Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, Logistics. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
Version 2.1, valid from 1 January 2025.

## 1. Delivery times

- Items in stock at the regional depot: delivery within 5 working days after the order is confirmed.
- Items not in stock: delivery within 15 working days after the order is confirmed. We confirm the date in writing
  within 3 working days.
- Back-ordered items: the customer is told the expected date when the order is confirmed. The date is an estimate.

## 2. Delivery windows

Deliveries take place Monday to Friday between 07:00 and 16:00. Crane lorry deliveries are booked in a 4-hour slot and must
be requested at least 72 hours in advance. Standard lorry deliveries are made on the confirmed day without a fixed window.

## 3. Unloading and waiting time

The first 45 minutes at the site are free. After that, a waiting charge of 70 euros per 30 minutes is applied. The customer
must make sure that access is clear, the ground is firm and a person is present to sign the delivery note.

## 4. Late delivery

If a delivery arrives later than the confirmed date, a credit of 1% of the order value is given for each working day of delay,
capped at 5%. The customer must claim within 20 working days of the delivery date.

## 5. Delivery note and checks

The driver hands over a delivery note listing each item and the quantity. The customer checks quantity and condition at
unloading. Damage or shortage must be written on the delivery note and reported to Customer Service within 5 working days.

## 6. Service level target

We target 90% of deliveries on time, measured quarterly. The result is reported to key customers on request.

## 7. Changes and cancellations

An order can be changed free of charge until 48 hours before dispatch. After that, a change fee of 25 euros applies.
Cancelling a crane lorry slot less than 48 hours ahead is charged at 100 euros.
