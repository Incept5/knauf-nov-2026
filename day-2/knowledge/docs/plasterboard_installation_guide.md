# Plasterboard Installation Guide (excerpt)

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop. Revision C, issued 2 March 2026.
This revision replaces revisions A and B.

## 1. Scope

This excerpt covers non-load-bearing partitions built with steel studs and 12.5 mm or 15 mm plasterboard on
sites in Germany and Austria. It does not cover shaft walls, curved walls or ceilings; see the separate guides.

## 2. Framing

Use galvanised steel studs of 50 mm or 75 mm width in matching floor and ceiling tracks. Fix the tracks every 600 mm.
Cut studs 10 mm shorter than the clear height so that the partition can move slightly.

The maximum stud spacing for a single-layer partition with standard board is 600 mm between stud centres.
Double-layer partitions also use 600 mm centres.

For fire-rated systems the spacing is tighter. System FR-90 (75 mm studs, double layer on both sides, type F board)
must be built with studs at 400 mm centres. Never widen the spacing of a fire-rated system on site, because the fire
test result is only valid for the tested layout.

## 3. Maximum partition height

With 50 mm studs at 600 mm centres the partition may be up to 3.0 m high. With 75 mm studs at 600 mm centres it may be
up to 4.5 m high. For fire-rated systems, take the height limit from the system certificate, not from this table.

## 4. Fixing the boards

Screw spacing is 250 mm along board edges and 300 mm in the field of the board. Use 25 mm screws for the first layer and
35 mm screws for the second layer. Drive screws so that the head sits just below the paper surface without breaking it.

Stagger vertical joints between layers by at least 400 mm. Keep a 10 mm gap between the board and the floor, and close
it later with a flexible sealant. Boards are fitted vertically unless the wall is wider than the board is tall.

## 5. Wet areas

Standard board is not suitable for permanently wet areas. In bathrooms and kitchens use a moisture-resistant board and
seal all cut edges. Do not install board that has been wet or has swollen edges; report it as damaged goods.

## 6. Jointing and finishing

Fill joints with jointing compound and leave 24 hours to dry before sanding. Do not install or finish if the room
temperature is below 5 degrees Celsius. Open buckets of compound must be closed after use and stored upright.

## 7. Handling on site

A 15 mm board of 1200 x 2000 mm weighs roughly 26 kg. Two people must carry it. Store boards flat on level ground and keep
them dry. Site stacking limits are defined in the site safety procedure, not in this guide.

## 8. Quality check before closing a wall

Before the second side is boarded, check that insulation is fitted without gaps, that services are in place and that
penetrations are sealed according to the fire-protection FAQ. Take a dated photo of each wall for the handover file.
