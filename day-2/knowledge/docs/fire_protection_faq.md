# Fire Protection Systems: Frequently Asked Questions

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, technical service. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
FAQ no. 12/2023. Last reviewed: May 2023.

## What fire resistance can I get with plasterboard?

Type F fire-rated board (15 mm) is used in tested systems. System FR-60 gives 60 minutes with a single layer of type F
board on each side of a steel stud frame. System FR-90 gives up to 90 minutes and needs a double layer of type F board
on both sides of a 75 mm steel stud frame. Only the complete system is tested; a board alone has no fire rating.

## What stud spacing should I use?

For all partitions, including fire-rated ones, the maximum stud spacing is 625 mm between centres. This allows a
standard 1250 mm wide board to be fixed on a stud at its edges and in the middle.

## Can I use standard board instead of type F?

No. Replacing even one layer of type F board with standard board voids the system rating. The same applies to using
thinner board or leaving out the insulation that the system certificate requires.

## How do I treat penetrations for pipes and cables?

Small penetrations up to 100 square centimetres may be sealed with mineral wool and an intumescent sealant on both sides of
the wall. Larger penetrations need a certified collar or a separate approval from the fire engineer. Never leave a
penetration open "until later". Record each sealed penetration in the handover file.

## Are fire-rated walls also sound-insulating?

Fire rating and sound insulation are separate properties. A FR-90 wall usually gives good sound insulation, but the
figure depends on insulation thickness and on flanking paths. Ask for the acoustic data sheet for your specific build-up.

## Who issues the certificate?

Each system has a test report from an accredited institute. The report number is quoted on the product datasheet. A site
may be asked to show that the built wall matches the tested system, so keep photos and delivery notes.

## How long does the fire rating last?

The rating is valid for the life of the building if the wall is not damaged or altered. After any cut-out or repair,
the repair must follow the repair detail of the system and be recorded.

## Where can I get more help?

Contact the technical service desk. Please have the system name, the wall height and the project reference ready. For
anything involving a live site safety concern, follow the site safety procedure first and call the technical service
afterwards.
