# Site Safety Procedure SP-07: Material Storage and Incident Reporting

Alpenbau Baustoffe GmbH, Health and Safety. FICTIONAL document for a training workshop.
Version 4, effective 15 June 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets the rules for storing building materials on site and for reporting incidents and near misses.
It applies to our own staff and to all subcontractors working on our sites.

## 2. Agreeing storage areas

The storage area for each level must be agreed at the morning briefing and written in the daily plan. Nobody may put
material down in an area that has not been agreed. If the plan changes during the day, the site manager must tell all
teams again.

## 3. Stacking rules on site

Pallets of boards and buckets may be stacked a maximum of 2 pallets high on site. Stacks must stand on level, firm
ground. Keep stacks at least 2 metres away from lift shafts, stair openings and open edges, and put a barrier or
tape around them. Never store material in a stairwell or escape route. Buckets of jointing compound must be stored
upright with lids closed and must not be stacked on uneven ground.

Warehouse limits are different and are set by the warehouse manager. They do not apply on site.

## 4. Reporting an incident

Anyone who sees an incident or a near miss must tell the site manager immediately.

1. Make the area safe first. Do not put yourself at risk.
2. Tell the site manager immediately, by phone or in person.
3. The site manager writes an incident report (form IR) within 24 hours.
4. For any injury, or a near miss that could have caused serious injury, the site manager also calls the H&S officer within 2 hours.
5. The H&S officer decides whether the insurer or the authority must be notified. An injury with more than 3 days of absence must be notified.

In an emergency call 112 first.

## 5. After the report

The report must name the contributing factors, the immediate actions and the open actions. Each open action has
one owner and a due date. The H&S officer reviews all open actions every Friday until they are closed.

## 6. Toolbox talks

After a storage-related incident, a toolbox talk on stacking and storage is held on that site within 7 days. Attendance is
recorded and kept for 3 years.

## 7. Personal protective equipment

Helmets, safety shoes and gloves are mandatory in all work areas. Dust masks are required when sanding joint compound.
Cut-resistant gloves are required when cutting board with a knife.
