PRODUCT DATA EXCERPT (fictional): Fire-rated plasterboard 15 mm, type F
Fire resistance: up to 90 min in system FR-90 with 75 mm steel studs at 400 mm centres, double layer both sides.
Not suitable for permanently wet areas without a moisture-resistant board. Maximum stud spacing for single layer: 600 mm.
Fixing: screws every 250 mm at board edges, 300 mm in the field. Store flat, dry, on level pallets; max 8 pallets high.
